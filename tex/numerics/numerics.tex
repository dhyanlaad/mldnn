\section{Numerical Experiments}

All stochastic results below were regenerated with random seed $42$, $R=500$
paired Brownian paths, $N^*=65{,}536$ fine increments, and $N_q=64$
collocation points.  Multiplicative-noise examples use the finite-dimensional
operator-trace Malliavin correction obtained by differentiating the full
least-squares normal equations; the trace is frozen for one corrected solve.
The initial condition is included as a weighted endpoint residual.  We report
$\mathcal E_\infty=\sup_t\operatorname{MSE}(t)$ and
$\mathcal E_2=\int_0^1\operatorname{MSE}(t)\,\dd t$.  ``Discrete $L_2$'' in
the mean tables denotes $N_e^{-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{N_e}
|\bar y_R(t_i)-y_{\rm ref}(t_i)|^2\right)^{1/2}$ on $N_e=101$ equally
spaced evaluation points; discrete $L_\infty$ is the corresponding maximum.

\begin{example}[Ornstein--Uhlenbeck Process]
\label{ex:linear_fractional_sde}
We first consider the general linear Caputo fractional stochastic differential equation with mean-reverting drift and additive diffusion:
\begin{equation}
    \DD y(t) = \theta(\mu - y(t)) + \sigma \sdv, \quad t \in [0, 1], \quad y(0) = y_0.
\end{equation}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Zero diffusion:} Let $\mu = \sigma = 0$, $\theta = 1.0$, and $y_0 = 1.0$. The equation reduces to the deterministic Caputo fractional differential equation $\DD y(t) = -y(t)$, with exact analytical solution $y(t) = E_\alpha(-t^\alpha)$.
